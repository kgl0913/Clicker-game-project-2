# Clicker-game-project-2
Made by Gia Linh Dang
Artistic statement:
1. I made a fantasy clicker game where players feel relaxed and at ease as they help an adorable cat alchemist brew magical potions to make coins. The game combines a charming, magical atmosphere with satisfying progression and rewarding coin rewards.
2. Game Element

Main Character (Cat Alchemist): 2D cat alchemist sprite, idle animation, potion-brewing animation, happy reaction animation, and character expressions.

Background: Cozy magical alchemy workshop, wooden shelves, potion bottles, books, magical plants, and a warm fireplace or glowing candles.

Clicking Mechanic: Clickable potion bottle or cauldron, clicking effects, magical sparkles, and floating ingredient icons.

Potions and Ingredients: Potion bottle sprites, herbs, mushrooms, crystals, magical flowers, and other collectible ingredients.

UI and Buttons: Fantasy-style buttons, ingredient counters, currency icons, upgrade buttons, inventory panel, and recipe book interface.

Animations and Effects: Bubbling potion effects, glowing particles, magical circles, smoke, sparkles, and potion-completion effects.

Audio: Rivetting background music, cat meows, potion bubbling, clicking sounds, magical chimes, and coin collection sounds.
